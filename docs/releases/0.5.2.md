# Nowspace 0.5.2 — Room to read, and a group that can change its day

Patch release: **no data-format change**, bucket schema stays at **3**, so
this installs alongside any other 0.5.x instance sharing the vault.

### Changed

- **Larger text takes a column away instead of squeezing three.** A day
  column narrower than about 120px can only show a word or two per line, so
  the multi-day views now give up columns rather than crush them: on a phone
  at 120% text and above, 3 Day shows two days; Mon-Fri narrows the same way
  where the screen calls for it. The View chip says which — "3 Day · 2" —
  rather than quietly showing something else. The days shown follow the day
  you selected, exactly as 3 Day has always worked.

- **A 2 Day view.** Two days side by side is the widest comparison a phone
  can actually render, and it's now a choice rather than a consequence. On
  small screens the View list offers Day, 2 Day, 3 Day and Weekend; Mon-Fri
  and Full week are hidden there, since neither has ever been readable at
  that width.

- **The day line stops wrapping.** The `weekday` / `weekend` chip is
  desktop-only now, and the task count reads "3 tasks" rather than "3 tasks
  for Fri". At a larger text size those two pushed the Notes button onto a
  second line — and the date immediately to their left already says both.

- **"+ Add task" moved to the top of the day.** The bottom of a full day is
  a scroll away on a phone, and a task you think of now shouldn't cost the
  journey. Double-click-to-add still works anywhere in the list.

### Added

- **A ＋ on each group header**, the same gesture as the Bucket's: adds
  straight into that group, no typing the `Group:` prefix.

- **The task you just added stays selected** — outlined, with its row
  actions showing — so the priority it deserves is one tap away instead of a
  hunt back down a re-sorted list.

- **Move a whole group to another day.** A → on the group header opens a day
  picker, and dragging a group header onto another day's column now works in
  the multi-day views. Both were refused before: the drag was rejected
  outright cross-day, and on a phone there is no drag at all, since a touch
  never starts one. Completed tasks stay on the day they were done — that
  day is a record, not a list.
