# Nowspace 0.5.7 — Room to write on a phone, without leaving the week

Patch release: **no data-format change**, bucket schema stays at **3**, so
this installs alongside any other 0.5.x instance sharing the vault.

### Changed

- **Opening Notes or Diary on a phone clears the furniture instead of
  moving you somewhere else.** The panel stays where it was — under the day,
  in the same window, a scroll away — because that's what lets you keep
  working with the tasks while you write, and what keeps links to the day's
  work reachable. What changes is everything around it: the pinned toolbar
  stops sticking and scrolls away with the page, and the floating corner
  buttons and the bottom bar step out. Both come back the moment the panel
  closes.

- **The same applies while you type anywhere in the week.** On a phone the
  toolbar, the week strip and the corner buttons take about a third of the
  screen, and the field you're typing into is what's left of it. They stand
  down while a field has focus and return on blur.

  Pinning stays your setting either way — this suspends it, it never
  overrides it, and only when the layout is stacked.

- **The notes panel starts closed on a phone.** It opened by default, which
  stacked under the day pushed the day's tasks off the bottom before you'd
  asked for it. Tapping Notes or Diary opens it and takes you down to it.
