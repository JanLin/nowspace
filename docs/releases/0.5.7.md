# Nowspace 0.5.7 — Room to type on a phone

Patch release: **no data-format change**, bucket schema stays at **3**, so
this installs alongside any other 0.5.x instance sharing the vault.

### Changed

- **The pinned toolbar and the floating buttons stand down while you type.**
  On a phone the toolbar, the week strip and the corner buttons together
  take a third of the screen, and the field you're typing into is what's
  left of it. While any field has focus the toolbar stops sticking and
  scrolls away with the page, and the corner buttons and bottom bar step
  out; everything comes back the moment you're done. Pinning is still your
  choice — this only suspends it, and only on a small screen.

- **Notes and Diary open as a sheet over the week on a phone.** They used to
  sit below the day, so writing meant scrolling the whole week out of the
  way and scrolling back to see the tasks — and the box was whatever height
  was left. On a small screen they now fill the screen, with a × back to the
  week; the box already shrinks to what the keyboard leaves visible, so the
  line being typed stays in view without scrolling the page behind it.
  Beside the tasks on anything wider, exactly as before.

- **They also start closed on a phone.** The notes panel opened by default,
  which on a small screen pushed the day's tasks off the bottom before you'd
  asked for it. It opens when you tap Notes or Diary.
