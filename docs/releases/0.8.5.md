# Nowspace 0.8.5 — The donut splits into sub-projects

**Patch release. No format change.**

### Changed

- **Tapping a company in the distribution now divides the ring into its
  sub-projects.** The caption has always said *"tap a slice to expand its
  sub-projects"*, and until now only the legend did — the ring stayed one
  undivided arc per company.

  Tap Arratech and its arc becomes `Arratech/CRA`, `Arratech/Peppol`,
  `Arratech (general)`, `Arratech/9001`, in shades of the company's own
  colour so it still reads as one company's time. Tap any of them again to
  fold back.

  A company whose time has never been split keeps its single arc — there is
  nothing to divide, and pretending otherwise would just redraw the same
  shape in a new colour.

  The Area view is unchanged.
