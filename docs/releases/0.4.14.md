## Nowspace 0.4.14 — drags reach past the edge

Patch release on top of 0.4.13, fully compatible with every 0.4.x (no
data-format change; the bucket schema stays at version 2).

### Fixed

- **A drag now scrolls when it reaches the edge of the screen.** Before, a
  drag could only reach what was already visible: to move a task to a day
  further down, or a group past the fold, you had to drop it somewhere,
  scroll, and pick it up again. Hold a dragged task near the top or bottom
  edge and the list scrolls under it, faster the closer you get to the rim.
  It applies to every drag — tasks, whole groups, bucket rows, carry rows,
  vault notes — and to panels with their own scrolling as well as the page.

### Known limit — dragging on a phone

This helps wherever dragging works, which on a touch screen is less than it
should be: Nowspace uses the browser's native drag-and-drop, and that never
starts from a touch on iOS, and only sometimes on Android. Nothing in
Nowspace is drag-only for this reason — a task's priority badge menu moves
it to another weekday, into the bucket at a horizon, or out again, and the
bucket and carry panels file items with a tap. Proper touch dragging is a
separate piece of work.

### macOS install note

If macOS reports the downloaded app as "damaged", it isn't — see the
README: after copying to /Applications, run
`xattr -cr /Applications/Nowspace.app` once.
