# Nowspace 0.7.1 — A tab that went missing now comes back on its own

**Patch release. No format change**, so instances on 0.7.0 and 0.7.1 share a
vault happily.

### Fixed

- **An extension tab could disappear until you reloaded the page.** Reported
  from the Android PWA: after the app had been left alone for a while, the
  Relay tab was gone; pulling to refresh brought it straight back.

  The tab is drawn from the vault settings — a surface whose switch is not in
  the answer is not rendered at all. That read happened exactly once. If it
  failed, nothing asked again for the rest of the session, so a single
  unlucky moment cost the tab until the page was reloaded by hand. A phone
  waking its radio, a Mac still starting its backend, the mini a second away
  over Tailscale: all of them produce that moment, which is why it happened
  "every so often" rather than always.

  The read is now retried until it lands — backing off to every 30 seconds,
  and asking straight away when you come back to the app or the network
  returns, which is both the likeliest moment for it to succeed and the
  moment a phone's throttled background timers start running again.

  A tab still never appears on a guess: while the settings genuinely cannot
  be read, it stays away. What changed is that the app keeps asking.

### Notes

Nothing to do on your side. The desktop app and the PWA pick this up the next
time they update.
